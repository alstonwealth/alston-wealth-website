How we work

A conversation, a written plan, then two reviews a year.

Most people work with us after one to three meetings. Here is what each step involves, and exactly how we are paid.

STEP ONE

A free call

30 to 45 minutes by video, and it's all about you. We want to understand your goals and assign every dollar a purpose. To give you a proper recommendation, we have to understand your specific situation, and that's exactly what this call is for.

STEP TWO

A written plan

If it makes sense to go further, we gather your statements — tax return, benefit statements, account statements — and build the plan from them. Every figure traces back to something you gave us. Where what you want and what your money supports disagree, we say so plainly. Sometimes, based on the data you've given us, we find a serious problem, and we'll let you know. Other times the answer is that you are fine and should change nothing, and we write that down too.

STEP THREE

Put it in place

We walk through what to do and in what order, and handle the paperwork with you. If we manage your investments, the accounts are opened in your name at Schwab or Fidelity.

EVERY YEAR

Two reviews, on purpose

Retirement has two natural decision points each year. In summer we plan: project your taxes, test spending against the plan, and set the year-end list. In the fall we act on that list before the December 31 deadlines. Between them you get a quarterly report, the monthly call, and a note from us whenever markets move sharply. Your plan is a living, breathing document, something we review as things change and update accordingly.

How we're paid

We would rather you hear this from us than wonder about it.

The first call is free, and so is everything we publish — the briefings, the tools and the monthly call.

When we manage your investments, we charge a yearly fee based on the amount we manage. It is billed every three months, taken from the account at the custodian, and shown on your statements.

Amount we manageYearly fee
First $1 million1.00%
$1 million to $3 million0.85%
$3 million to $5 million0.70%
Above $5 million0.55%

Each rate applies only to the portion in that range. The minimum yearly fee per household is $6,000. Planning, tax work and the two reviews are included.

When an insurance contract is part of your plan, the insurance company pays a commission. It does not come out of your money. It is a real conflict of interest and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise — it is why we lay out the alternatives side by side, including the ones that pay us nothing.

Questions about fees?

Ask on the first call. We will tell you what working together would cost for your situation before you decide anything.
