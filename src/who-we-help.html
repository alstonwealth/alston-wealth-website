After losing a spouse

When a spouse dies, household income usually falls — one Social Security check stops and some pension income may stop with it. Taxes often rise at the same time, because a single filer reaches higher brackets sooner.

There is a lot to sort, and very little of it has to be decided in the first weeks. We help separate what needs doing now from what can wait, and then build a plan for the income you have.

If you are married and planning ahead, this is worth looking at together, now.

Planning what you leave behind

Some families will never spend most of what they have. The income question is settled; the open questions are about who gets what, how it gets there, and how much the tax bill takes on the way.

We check that the names on every account match your wishes, plan around the taxes your heirs will owe, and work alongside your estate attorney so the documents and the money agree.
