How each figure is calculated

Projected account value
The balance is compounded annually. With end-of-year contributions, the year's growth equals the beginning balance times the annual return, and the contribution is added after growth is applied. With beginning-of-year contributions, the contribution is added first and the full amount grows for the year.
Total contributions
The annual contribution multiplied by the number of years. The starting balance is not counted as a contribution.
Total investment growth
The projected account value less the starting balance and less total contributions. It also equals the sum of the growth column in the table.
Estimated taxes deferred
Cumulative pre-tax contributions multiplied by the combined federal and state tax rate entered for today.
Estimated future tax liability
The projected account balance multiplied by the combined federal and state tax rate entered for withdrawal.
Estimated client after-tax value
The projected account balance less the estimated future tax liability.
Ownership percentages
The client percentage is the after-tax value divided by the projected balance. The tax percentage is the future tax liability divided by the projected balance, which equals the combined future tax rate.

What this calculator does not account for

  • Graduated tax brackets
  • Deductions or credits
  • Required minimum distributions
  • Withdrawal timing
  • Social Security taxation
  • Medicare IRMAA
  • Changes in tax law
  • State tax deductibility
  • Tax-free basis
  • Employer matching contributions, unless entered as part of the annual contribution

A single flat rate is applied to the entire balance rather than to distributions as they occur. Actual results depend on future tax law, the timing and size of withdrawals, and each individual's full tax picture.
